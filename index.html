Who we are

A safe place where every child is seen, guided and valued.

Visvatha Special School was founded on a simple belief — every child learns differently, and every child deserves an environment built around their pace, not the other way around. Our team of special educators, therapists and caregivers work together to build individualised plans that nurture academic, emotional and life skills side by side.

Located in Korattur, Chennai, our classrooms are designed to be calm, sensory-friendly and joyful — a place where progress is celebrated in every small step.

Our approach

Why families choose Visvatha

Individualised Education Plans

Every child gets a personalised plan shaped by their strengths, needs and pace of learning.

Small, Nurturing Classrooms

Consistent, Structured Routines

Predictable daily rhythms that help children feel secure and confident.

Sensory-Friendly Spaces
